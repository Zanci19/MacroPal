How to Test

  1. Build the app with VITE_DEMO_MODE=true in your .env file
  2. Place a video file at /public/assets/demo-loop.mp4
  3. Open the app in a browser on a landscape monitor (e.g., 1920x1080)
  4. Observe the video plays in landscape with subtle overlay text (50% opacity)
  5. Click anywhere to transition to the app
  6. App should appear in portrait mode (9:16) with black borders on sides
